### Online Repository: Chatbots in Education: A Discussion of Stakeholders, Requirements, and Solutions

This is the online repository for the paper *Chatbots in Education: A Discussion of Stakeholders, Requirements, and Solutions*. 

You can find the two datasets (main, critical) in folder *data*.

The codebook can be found in Coedbook.pdf.

Further analyses (as referenced in the paper but also additional ones) can be found in AddtitionalAnalysis.pdf.
